Northtown NHS Foundation Trust
Data Analyst (Band 5)

We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our informatics team, supporting
performance reporting across several clinical directorates.

Essential Criteria
- Experience using SQL to extract and manipulate data
- Strong Excel skills, including pivot tables and formulas
- Experience building dashboards or reports (e.g. Power BI)
- Ability to communicate data insights to non-technical stakeholders
- Educated to degree level or equivalent experience

Desirable Criteria
- Experience working with healthcare or NHS data
- Familiarity with Python for data analysis
- Knowledge of NHS data quality standards

Key Responsibilities
- Produce regular performance and quality reports for clinical teams
- Maintain and improve existing dashboards
- Investigate and resolve data quality issues at source
- Support ad-hoc analysis requests from service leads

Values and Behaviours
- Patient and public focus in all analytical work
- Strong teamwork and collaboration across departments
- Commitment to continuous improvement
